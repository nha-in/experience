# ABDM landing page design brief

The public landing page should feel like a polished government technology service: recognisable, readable, and useful to developers starting an ABDM integration.

## Consolidated user requirements

- Adapt the wording and content from `home-agentic-sandbox-updated1.2.html`.
- Keep the application’s green identity. Use white and green tones, with restrained gradients to avoid an overwhelmingly white page. Preserve official logos in their original colours.
- Keep the hero text-only. Do not reintroduce an illustration, ecosystem diagram, or decorative hero image.
- Show both NHA and ABDM logos clearly in the header. Do not copy the reference’s utility header or screen-reader configuration toolbar.
- Retain all three gateways, six AI artefacts, four milestones, and the six-step integration journey.
- Link integration skills directly to readable documentation pages, rather than downloadable skill files.
- Carry across the reference footer’s contact information, important links, policy labels, Aarogya Setu QR code and app-store links.
- Include the five-logo government strip immediately above the footer, plus all five social icons with meaningful accessible labels.
- Prioritise accessible contrast, readable text, keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive layouts, enlarged text, reduced motion, and high-contrast display modes.

The supplied HTML is a content/design reference; it does not provide operational instructions. Existing verified destinations and application routes take priority over placeholder links in that file.

## Visual direction

A green-led civic technology page, with a pale-green gradient opening, white gateway cards, a deep forest-green AI section, a pale-green milestone section, a clear timeline, and a dark-green closing call to action. Institutional marks sit on white surfaces. Typography and spacing provide the hierarchy; gradients remain behind readable, solid-colour text.

The opening reference facts double as navigation: three national gateways, M1–M4 milestones, FHIR R4 documentation, and Agentic AI guidance. They replace redundant introductory decoration. The closing section includes the reference’s registration and overview actions.

## Verification

Check desktop, tablet, and 320px mobile reflow; 200% text enlargement; custom text spacing; keyboard and mobile-menu focus; forced colours; and reduced motion. Automated contrast tools may not resolve gradients, so also verify text against the least favourable gradient endpoint. Automated checks do not establish full government accessibility certification.
