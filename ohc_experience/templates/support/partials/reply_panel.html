{% comment %} The reply box and the one status move a vendor may make. Two sibling forms, never nested: the reply posts to support:reply, the status button posts to support:status. "Send reply" sits in the second row beside "Mark as resolved" but belongs to the first form — the form= attribute is what associates them, which needs no script and keeps the mockup's layout without putting a form inside a form. The status button is the whole choice a vendor gets: resolve an open ticket, or reopen one. Closing is the Care team's, and the view's form refuses it. Both forms keep method, action and a CSRF token, so with scripting off they POST and the views redirect back to this thread. hx-post adds the entry to the end of the conversation instead, with this panel, the badges and the details rail returning out of band.
